## Platform

web

## Users

Three B2B audiences in the Philippines, all in or reaching Calabarzon:

1. **Aspiring laundry entrepreneurs**: people who want to start a laundry business and need equipment plus a plan.
2. **Existing laundry operators**: owners who already run a laundry business and want to upgrade equipment or operations.
3. **Hospitality operators**: hotels, motels and similar businesses that want to run a laundry operation inside their own property.

None of these are laundry customers dropping off clothes. They are buyers making a capital-equipment and business-setup decision.

## Product Purpose

TeamG Venturez sells and supports commercial laundry operations. It is the exclusive distributor of Samsung laundry equipment in Calabarzon, Philippines, and helps clients build an end-to-end operation rather than just buy machines. Success is a qualified inquiry from an owner, operator or hospitality business that wants to start or upgrade a laundry operation.

## Positioning

- **Public wording (decided 2026-11-26): "authorized Samsung dealer".** The exclusive-distributor claim above is the owner's stated position, but no distributor letter is on file, so the site does not print it until the owner confirms it in writing.
- **End-to-end partner.** Design, construction, equipment installation and ongoing maintenance, plus point-of-sale systems and business and marketing consultation.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the exclusive Samsung distributorship in Calabarzon, the one claim no competitor can make.
2. Present the business as an end-to-end partner (design to maintenance), not an equipment catalog.
3. Speak to three distinct buyers (start-ups, upgraders, hospitality) with their own entry points.
4. Treat every inquiry as a high-value lead: make contacting TeamG easy through form, phone, text or email.
5. Never state prices, models, clients or credentials that the owner has not confirmed.
