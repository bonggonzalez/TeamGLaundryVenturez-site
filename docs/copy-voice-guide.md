# TeamG Venturez: voice and terms guide (DRAFT for owner edit)

Source: the owner's feedback on sample lines. Every reviewer and every future copy edit is judged against this.

## Voice
1. **We and us, never "TeamG" in the third person.** Say "Three ways to work with us", not "...with TeamG". Use "you" for the reader. The brand name appears in titles, the logo and legal lines.
2. **Warm and relationship-first.** Filipino buyers want a person they can trust before a transaction. Write like a courteous, knowledgeable dealer speaking to a customer, not like a spec sheet or a ticket system.
3. **No clipped "We do X. You do Y." chains.** Blunt fragments such as "You buy from us. We install it. We keep it running." sound cold, transactional and a little curt. Join the ideas into one friendly sentence that states what the reader gets.
4. **No slogan fragments** such as "One team. First plan to first service." Say the benefit in a full sentence.
5. **English by default.** B2B web copy stays in English. Light Taglish is allowed only where it is natural and warm, for example "laundry negosyo" (approved by the owner). Do not translate headlines into Tagalog, and do not force Tagalog everywhere.
6. **Respectful, not salesy.** No hype, no pressure, no claims we cannot prove.

## Terms
| Avoid | Use instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| motel / motels | BnB (also: hotel, inn, transient home, resort, as accurate) | In the Philippines "motel" implies short-stay lodging and carries a bad connotation. |
| "TeamG" (third person, in body copy) | we / us / our team | Too impersonal. |
| "You buy from us. We install it." style chains | one flowing sentence about what they get | Reads curt. |
| Cute or abstract section labels | headings that say what the section delivers | Clarity. |
