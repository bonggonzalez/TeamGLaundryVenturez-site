## B. Add content: needs the owner's facts [CONFIRM]

These were the top gaps for first-time buyers and shop owners. Right now they all end in "speak to us", which Filipino buyers read as evasive.

1. **Reply time and hours** on contact lede, success message and FAQ ("We reply within [CONFIRM] ..."; which channel is fastest; days and hours).
2. **Warranty, financing/hulugan, Samsung Care+** (faq:12, faq:21). Who honors the warranty, how long, any installment terms, or plainly say there are none.
3. **Project timeline** (faq:20): typical weeks for a starter shop.
4. **Service and parts**: response time per province, local parts stock, hotline hours. Add a "Service and parts" block on services and an FAQ "How fast do you respond when a machine stops?".
5. **Space, power, water, drainage, ventilation, permits** ("What your space needs"; minimum floor area for a starter set). Also "Can I run this on my power connection?"
6. **Budget and payback**: price range or "tell us your budget and we'll plan around it"; whether you help estimate loads per day and payback.
7. **Hospitality for BnB scale** (packages:33-44): rename to "Hotels, BnBs and Inns"; add linens, room count, a compact setup and the king-size duvet capacity from products:17. [CONFIRM smallest property or machine count served; whether a single washer-dryer pair is offered.]
8. **Proof of being real**: showroom address, DTI/SEC number, named team, year appointed, 1-2 real installs with consent, Facebook link. Every image is currently captioned "for illustration", which reads as stock photos.
9. **Dryer-to-washer ratio**: the 14 kg dryer is the bottleneck against the 18 kg washer; add "How many dryers do I need per washer?".
10. **Service area**: optionally list key cities; add "Other area" to the form's province field.
11. **Full model range**: faq:19 says "ask us" while products lists only two. Say these are the two models, or list the others.

## C. Claims and compliance (owner and Samsung must confirm)

- "Exclusive distributor" appears about 10 times with no scope or proof. [CONFIRM: agreement wording, territory, product lines.] Safer if unconfirmed: "Authorized Samsung Professional Laundry distributor for Calabarzon".
- No trademark line in the footer. Proposed: "Samsung and Samsung Professional Laundry are trademarks of Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. Used with permission." [CONFIRM Samsung brand rules.]
- Product images and spec numbers were sourced from a third-party site (SOURCES.md). [CONFIRM Samsung permission; verify specs against the Samsung PH datasheet.]
- "Tested to 30,000 cycles": add source, which parts, and drop "Excellent". Add the asterisk to the spec-table cycle times, which currently have none.
- "Open API", "Wi-Fi embedded", auto-dosing compatibility, Care+ and payment systems (coin, RFID, QR, card): confirm they are active and supported locally. "Card" and "credit card" overlap in faq:15 and products:23.
- Check the logo asset is the current official one.

## D. Conversion and form microcopy

- Hero: add a "Message us on Viber" action; ContactBar and contact page should lead with chat. Label channels "Chat on Viber" etc.
- Form: if `PUBLIC_FORM_ENDPOINT` is empty the form opens an email app, which many Android phones lack. Set the endpoint before launch.
- Form labels: email and last name "(optional)"; selects need a "Choose one" default; "Location" becomes "Which province is your laundry in?"; contact:105 placeholder asks for floor area, so add "or a rough idea" and "number of rooms / loads per day".
- Success and error messages: "Salamat po!" stays (approved warm Taglish); add the reply time; error: "Sorry, we could not send your request. Please try again, or call or text us at ...".
- Vary closing CTAs ("Ask us a question" on FAQ; "Tell us about your plan").
- SEO: packages, about, faq and contact titles lack keywords; Layout default description is just "TeamG Venturez"; products meta is thin. Index meta is about 158 characters, so trim it.
- Jargon for first-timers: explain or drop "Open API", "DD Motor", "VRT+", "SKK-BC/SKK-CC1" (keep part codes on products only), "Point-of-sale" ("coin, card or QR payment and a sales record so you can see your daily income"), and "Drum volume 158 L" next to "18 kg".

## E. Terms proposed for the guide (see guide, "Proposed additions")

operators → owners; premises → property or space; odor → smell; first payment → opening day; "Speak to us" on price or terms → "Message us and we will walk you through it" (once facts exist); "rented shop" → "shop space"; tighten/optimize → make it run more smoothly; "straight from" / "Buy from X" → "from us".
